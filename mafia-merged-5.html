How to Play
The Social Deduction Game
🎭 The Roles
🔴
Mafia — Know each other. Kill one citizen per night. Survive until equal in number to citizens.
👤
Citizen — Discuss and vote to eliminate suspects each day. Find the Mafia!
💊
Doctor — Each night, choose one player to protect from elimination. Can save themselves.
🔍
Detective — Each night, investigate one player and learn if they are Mafia or not.
👑
Godfather — Mafia leader. Appears innocent to the Detective's investigation.
🎯
Vigilante — Citizens' secret weapon. Can eliminate one player per game during the night.
🌙 Night Phase
01
Everyone closes their eyes.
02
Mafia silently chooses a victim to eliminate.
03
Doctor chooses someone to protect.
04
Detective secretly investigates one player.
☀ Day Phase
01
The game reveals who was eliminated (or saved).
02
All players discuss and debate who the Mafia might be.
03
Players vote to eliminate a suspect. Majority rules.
🏆 Win Conditions
🔴
Mafia wins when Mafia members equal or outnumber remaining citizens.
✅
Citizens win when all Mafia members are eliminated.
